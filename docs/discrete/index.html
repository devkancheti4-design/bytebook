Discrete Mathematics

The mathematics under computer science: logic, proofs, sets, relations, functions, counting, recurrences, graph theory, number theory and Boolean algebra.

What you will be able to do

  • Build truth tables and simplify logical expressions
  • Write direct, contradiction and induction proofs
  • Work with sets, relations, functions and partial orders
  • Count with permutations, combinations and inclusion–exclusion
  • Solve recurrence relations
  • Reason about graphs, trees, modular arithmetic and Boolean algebra

Syllabus

Unit 1Logic & Proofs

  1. 1Propositional LogicPropositions and the logical connectives — negation, AND, OR, XOR, implication and biconditional — with truth tables, converse and contrapositive, and translating English.4 min
  2. 2Logical Equivalences & Normal FormsWhen two formulas mean the same thing: the laws of logic including De Morgan's, simplifying expressions step by step, and converting to disjunctive and conjunctive normal form.4 min
  3. 3Predicates & QuantifiersStatements with variables: predicates, the universal and existential quantifiers, domains, negating quantified statements, nested quantifiers and translating English.4 min
  4. 4Proof TechniquesHow mathematicians and computer scientists prove things: rules of inference, direct proof, proof by contrapositive, contradiction, cases, counterexamples, and existence proofs.4 min
  5. 5Mathematical InductionProve statements for all natural numbers: the base case and inductive step, sums, divisibility and inequalities, strong induction, and the link to recursion and loop invariants.5 min

Unit 2Sets, Relations & Functions

  1. 6SetsSets and their notation, subsets and power sets, union, intersection, difference and complement with Venn diagrams, set identities, Cartesian products and cardinality.5 min
  2. 7RelationsRelations as sets of ordered pairs, matrix and digraph representations, the reflexive, symmetric, antisymmetric and transitive properties, equivalence relations and classes, and closures.4 min
  3. 8Partial Orders & LatticesOrdering things that are not all comparable: posets, Hasse diagrams, maximal and minimal elements, upper and lower bounds, lattices and topological sorting.3 min
  4. 9FunctionsFunctions as special relations: domain, codomain and range, one-to-one, onto and bijective functions, composition, inverses, floor and ceiling, and counting functions.3 min

Unit 3Counting

  1. 10Counting Principles & PigeonholeThe sum and product rules, counting with complements, tree diagrams, and the pigeonhole principle with its generalised form and classic applications.4 min
  2. 11Permutations & CombinationsOrdered arrangements and unordered selections: nPr and nCr, factorials, repetition, arrangements with identical items, circular permutations and stars and bars.3 min
  3. 12Inclusion–Exclusion & the Binomial TheoremCount unions without double counting using inclusion–exclusion, apply it to divisibility and derangements, and expand (x + y)ⁿ with the binomial theorem and Pascal's triangle.4 min
  4. 13Recurrence RelationsDefine sequences recursively, model counting problems with recurrences, and solve linear homogeneous recurrences with constant coefficients using the characteristic equation.4 min

Unit 4Graphs, Number Theory & Algebra

  1. 14Graph TheoryGraphs as mathematics: simple graphs and multigraphs, degree and the handshake lemma, special graphs, isomorphism, Euler and Hamilton paths, planarity and graph colouring.5 min
  2. 15TreesTrees in discrete mathematics: characterisations, rooted and m-ary trees with their counting formulas, spanning trees, Cayley's formula and prefix codes.4 min
  3. 16Number TheoryDivisibility, primes and the fundamental theorem of arithmetic, GCD and LCM with Euclid's algorithm, modular arithmetic, congruences, modular inverses, fast exponentiation and an RSA preview.5 min
  4. 17Boolean AlgebraThe algebra of 0 and 1 behind every circuit: Boolean operations and laws, duality, Boolean functions, sum-of-products and product-of-sums forms, logic gates and Karnaugh-map simplification.4 min
  5. 18Discrete Mathematics Formula SheetEvery rule, law and formula from Discrete Mathematics on one page — logic, proofs, sets, relations, functions, counting, recurrences, graphs, trees, number theory and Boolean algebra.3 min
