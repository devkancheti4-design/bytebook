Design and Analysis of Algorithms

Analyse and design efficient algorithms: asymptotic analysis, recurrences, divide and conquer, greedy methods, dynamic programming, backtracking, graph algorithms and NP-completeness.

What you will be able to do

  • Analyse running time with O, Ω and Θ
  • Solve recurrences with the Master Theorem
  • Design divide-and-conquer and greedy algorithms
  • Solve problems with dynamic programming tables
  • Use backtracking and branch and bound
  • Explain P, NP, NP-complete and NP-hard

Syllabus

Unit 1Analysis of Algorithms

  1. 1What Is an Algorithm?The definition and five properties of an algorithm, how to write pseudocode, proving correctness with loop invariants, and the RAM model used to analyse running time.4 min
  2. 2Asymptotic NotationsThe formal definitions of O, Ω and Θ (plus little-o and little-ω), proving bounds by finding c and n₀, the order of growth, and the properties exams test.4 min
  3. 3Analysing Loops & Recursive CodeTurn code into running times: independent and dependent nested loops, logarithmic loops, writing recurrences for recursive functions, and an introduction to amortised analysis.5 min
  4. 4Solving Recurrences & the Master TheoremSolve recurrences with the substitution method, recursion trees and the Master Theorem — with its three cases, worked examples, and the cases it cannot handle.4 min

Unit 2Divide and Conquer

  1. 5Divide and ConquerThe divide, conquer, combine paradigm with full analysis of binary search, merge sort and quick sort, including quick sort's worst case and randomised pivots.5 min
  2. 6More Divide and ConquerMax–min with fewer comparisons, counting inversions with merge sort, Karatsuba's fast multiplication and Strassen's matrix multiplication.6 min

Unit 3Greedy Algorithms

  1. 7The Greedy MethodMake the locally best choice at every step: the greedy-choice property, activity selection, fractional knapsack, job sequencing with deadlines, and when greedy fails.5 min
  2. 8Huffman CodingOptimal prefix-free codes for data compression: why prefix codes, building the Huffman tree with a min-priority queue, reading off codes, and computing the compressed size.4 min
  3. 9Minimum Spanning TreesConnect every vertex at minimum total cost: spanning trees, Kruskal's algorithm with union-find, Prim's algorithm with a priority queue, traced on the same graph.4 min
  4. 10Shortest Paths: Dijkstra & Bellman–FordSingle-source shortest paths in weighted graphs: relaxation, Dijkstra's greedy algorithm with a priority queue, why it fails on negative edges, and Bellman–Ford with negative-cycle detection.4 min

Unit 4Dynamic Programming

  1. 11Dynamic ProgrammingSolve each subproblem once: overlapping subproblems and optimal substructure, memoisation vs tabulation, and DP solutions for Fibonacci and coin change.5 min
  2. 120/1 KnapsackChoose whole items to maximise value within a weight limit: the DP table, the recurrence, tracing back the chosen items, and a space-optimised version.4 min
  3. 13Longest Common SubsequenceFind the longest sequence common to two strings: subsequences vs substrings, the LCS table and recurrence, traceback, and the related edit-distance problem.4 min
  4. 14Matrix Chain MultiplicationFind the cheapest way to parenthesise a product of matrices: why order matters, the m[i][j] recurrence, filling the table by chain length, and printing the optimal parenthesisation.3 min
  5. 15All-Pairs Shortest Paths: Floyd–WarshallShortest distances between every pair of vertices with a three-loop DP, its recurrence over intermediate vertices, negative-cycle detection, and Warshall's transitive closure.4 min

Unit 5Search, Graphs & Complexity

  1. 16BacktrackingExplore a state-space tree and abandon dead ends early: the N-Queens problem, subset sum, graph colouring and Hamiltonian cycles.5 min
  2. 17Branch and BoundOptimisation by bounding: FIFO, LIFO and least-cost branch and bound, solving 0/1 knapsack with an upper bound, and the travelling salesperson problem.5 min
  3. 18Topological Sort & ConnectivityOrder tasks with dependencies using Kahn's algorithm and DFS, find connected and strongly connected components, and detect cycles in directed graphs.4 min
  4. 19String MatchingFind a pattern in a text: the naive algorithm, Rabin–Karp's rolling hash, and the Knuth–Morris–Pratt algorithm with its prefix function — with complexities.5 min
  5. 20P, NP, NP-Complete & NP-HardTractable and intractable problems: decision problems, the classes P and NP, polynomial-time reductions, NP-complete and NP-hard problems, Cook's theorem, and coping with hardness.4 min
  6. 21Algorithms Cheat SheetDesign and Analysis of Algorithms on one page: notations, recurrences, the Master Theorem, every algorithm's complexity, technique selection and exam favourites.3 min
