What you will be able to do

  • Measure code with Big-O time and space complexity
  • Implement linked lists, stacks and queues from scratch
  • Build and traverse binary trees, BSTs, AVL trees and heaps
  • Represent graphs and run BFS and DFS
  • Use hashing and resolve collisions
  • Pick the right searching and sorting algorithm

Syllabus

Unit 1Foundations

  1. 1Introduction to Data StructuresWhat a data structure is, abstract data types, linear vs non-linear structures, and why choosing well matters more than coding fast.4 min
  2. 2Time & Space ComplexityCount steps instead of seconds: Big-O notation, the common growth rates, analysing loops, and best, average and worst cases.4 min
  3. 3Arrays as a Data StructureArrays from the data-structure point of view: O(1) access, the cost of insertion and deletion by shifting, and when an array is the right choice.4 min

Unit 2Linked Lists

  1. 4Singly Linked ListNodes joined by pointers: create, traverse, insert at the head, tail or a position, delete, and search — with every operation's cost.5 min
  2. 5Linked List ProblemsThe classic interview and exam problems: reverse a list, find the middle, detect a cycle with Floyd's algorithm, merge two sorted lists and remove duplicates.6 min
  3. 6Doubly & Circular Linked ListsLists that go both ways or loop around: node structure, insertion and deletion in a doubly linked list, circular lists, and when to use each.4 min

Unit 3Stacks & Queues

  1. 7StackLast in, first out: push, pop and peek, overflow and underflow, and two implementations — with an array and with a linked list.3 min
  2. 8Stack ApplicationsBalanced brackets, infix to postfix conversion and postfix evaluation — the three stack algorithms every CSE 102 exam asks you to trace.6 min
  3. 9Queue & Circular QueueFirst in, first out: enqueue and dequeue, why a simple array queue wastes space, and how the circular queue fixes it — plus a linked-list queue.4 min
  4. 10Deque & Priority QueueTwo useful queue variations: the double-ended queue and the priority queue, with array-based implementations and where each one shines.3 min

Unit 4Trees

  1. 11Trees & Binary TreesHierarchical data: tree vocabulary, types of binary trees, array and linked representations, and the four traversals — inorder, preorder, postorder and level order.5 min
  2. 12Binary Search TreeA binary tree that keeps itself sorted: search, insert, find min and max, and the three cases of deletion — plus why balance matters.5 min
  3. 13AVL TreesSelf-balancing BSTs: balance factors, the four rotation cases (LL, RR, LR, RL), and an insertion that stays O(log n) whatever order the keys arrive in.5 min
  4. 14Heaps & Heap SortComplete binary trees stored in arrays: max-heaps and min-heaps, insert with sift-up, extract with sift-down, building a heap in O(n), and heap sort.5 min

Unit 5Graphs & Hashing

  1. 15Graphs: Terminology & RepresentationVertices and edges: directed, weighted and connected graphs, degree, paths and cycles, and storing graphs as adjacency matrices and adjacency lists.4 min
  2. 16Graph Traversal: BFS & DFSExplore a graph level by level with a queue (BFS) or as deep as possible with a stack or recursion (DFS) — with step-by-step traces, shortest paths and applications.4 min
  3. 17HashingNear O(1) search with hash tables: hash functions, collisions, separate chaining, linear and quadratic probing, double hashing and the load factor.5 min

Unit 6Searching & Sorting

  1. 18Linear & Binary SearchFind things fast: linear search for any list, binary search for sorted ones — iterative and recursive versions, step-by-step traces and the classic off-by-one traps.5 min
  2. 19Bubble, Selection & Insertion SortThe three O(n²) sorts: how each one works, passes and swaps traced by hand, stability, best cases, and when a simple sort is the right choice.4 min
  3. 20Merge, Quick & Counting SortFaster sorting: divide-and-conquer merge sort and quick sort with their partition traces, plus counting and radix sort that beat O(n log n) on integers.5 min
  4. 21Data Structures Cheat SheetEvery structure and algorithm from CSE 102 on one page: operation costs, when to use what, key formulas and the programs to practise.4 min
